# Usability Testing Insights

Seven recurring insights emerged from grouping the usability testing notes using affinity diagramming. Each table shows the specific participant observations supporting that insight.

## Insight 1 — The steps describe a process that clicking through doesn't follow

| Participant | Note title | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Participant 1 – Claude | Hero CTAs + individual card buttons | Confusion between the 3 hero CTAs that lead to the form, and the button on each animal card that also leads to the same form. |
| Participant 1 – Claude | System steps vs. expected process (synthesis) | "The steps do not match the process I did throughout the app." |
| Participant 1 – Claude | 'Submit another application' button | She expected that button to take her back to the dog listing, not show the general form again — breaks her mental model. |
| Participant 1 – V0 | Two paths to the form | Same problem as in Claude — not clear whether clicking a specific dog means applying for that dog or a general adoption. |
| Participant 2 – V0 | 'How adoption works' steps vs. real experience | The described process doesn't match what she experienced — clicking an animal took her straight to the form. |
| Participant 2 – Claude | Form contradicts the 'how it works' section | The form says a shelter will reach out in two days — contradicting the process section above. "Learned something that was wrong." |

## Insight 2 — Looks trustworthy at first glance, but that trust falls apart once noticing there is no organizational information, testimonials or other expected sections

| Participant | Note title | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Participant 1 – V0 | Closing question — 'anything you'd add?' | "At first glance, the design feels trustworthy. But I am missing data to trust the organization. I don't know who they are, there is not About section, I would like to see testimonials." |
| Participant 2 – V0 | General first impression | "It feels like a catalog of products, as if I would be buying a toy." |
| Participant 2 – V0 | Trust — organization information | Not having more information about the organization doesn't build her trust. She only sees KPIs. |
| Participant 2 – Claude / final comparison | Closing question — V0 vs. Claude | "In both landing pages the adoption process felt confusing, and it doesn't give me confidence that adopting an animal is so easy such as filling out a form. They are animals." |

## Insight 3 — A giant hero and stats block come first, the actual adoption process comes much later

| Participant | Note title | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Participant 1 – Claude | First impression (Hero) | Hero and stats block felt oversized; "breaks the flow between entering the site and choosing an animal." |
| Participant 1 – V0 | Position of 'How adoption works' | She expected it up in the hero, not so far down after she'd already started browsing. |
| Participant 2 – V0 | Placement of important information | Expected the adoption process higher up; "the hero has a lot of space with descriptions that don't say much." |
| Participant 2 – Claude | First impression | First thing she sees is the wide black KPI block, before anything related to the real process. |

## Insight 4 — Missing the animal details, filters, and shelter locations you'd need to actually decide

| Participant | Note title | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Participant 1 – V0 | Animal listing | "I expected more information of the animal. Such as temperament, size, more characteristics." |
| Participant 1 – V0 | Filters | Expected to filter by animal type and by her own characteristics (house/apartment). |
| Participant 2 – V0 | Animal catalog | "I would like to see more information of the animal, their story, for example." |
| Participant 2 – Claude | Map and shelters | She'd like to see a map or list of shelters and where they're located. |

## Insight 5 — The top menu doesn't have FAQ, and 'About' doesn't lead to the organization

| Participant | Note title | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Participant 1 – Claude | Navigation — searching for FAQ | "I had to scroll down and find it, I expected it at the top menu." |
| Participant 1 – V0 | Top menu — 'About' option | Expected it to lead to organization info, but goes directly to the form. |
| Participant 2 – V0 | Navigation menu — 'About' | No 'About' section; clicking it leads to the form, same with 'Adopt' and 'How it works'. |
| Participant 2 – Claude | Findability task — looking for FAQ | Would look for it in the header menu, but it's not there. |

## Insight 6 — Wrong phone number, no warning, no way to recover

| Participant | Note title | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Participant 1 – Claude | Phone number error | "It didn't warn me that the number was wrong." No way to recover from the error. |
| Participant 2 – V0 | Induced error (invalid phone number) | Didn't warn her the number was wrong, allowed submission without blocking or correcting it. |
| Participant 2 – Claude | Same induced error (letter in number) | Same problem as V0 — no warning, no recovery. |

## Insight 7 — Badges and labels look clickable or meaningful, but they don't do anything

| Participant | Note title | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Participant 1 – Claude | '98% match' badge over hero photo | "I don't get what this is." |
| Participant 1 – Claude | 'How it works' step 2 | "I thought it was a button that would take me to the step, not just information." |
| Participant 1 – V0 | Label over the hero | Background gives the impression of a button/CTA, but it's not clickable. |
| Participant 2 – Claude | '95% match' badge in the hero | She finds it strange — tried to click on it, doesn't understand what it's for. |
